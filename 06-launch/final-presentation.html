05 · Strategy

Problem, Value, Hypothesis

PROBLEM HOOK

We must solve the administrative burden to reduce the support task for frontline coordinators.

VALUE PROPOSITION

Velocity gives coordinators one focused, real-time view — live vehicle status, exceptions, and one-tap reroute — so the tool becomes the fastest way to run the shift instead of the slowest.

Data-Backed Hypothesis

Based on reassignments taking 8–15 minutes to reach drivers with no notification, status updates lagging 20–60 minutes behind reality, and CSAT scores of 2.2 (Compliance) and 1.3 (Reporting) for Coordinators — I believe that real-time dashboard reflection and instant reassignment notifications for Coordinators and Drivers will result in on-time deliveries, avoided reroutes, and more completed deliveries per day, as measured by a 50–75% change in Assign Routes & Compliance Checks. We will protect the benchmark timeframe for Assigned Route and Compliance Checks, and make a go/no-go decision after optimizing the workflow and resolving the underlying communication-speed gap.

06 · Research

Competitive Analysis & Journey Map

Role
The Dispatcher — coordinates driver routes and monitors delivery status from a central dashboard.
Goal
Wants to trust that what the dashboard shows reflects reality, so they can coordinate the fleet without double-checking everything manually.
Moment of Misery
Reassignments take 8–15 minutes to reach drivers with no notification. Status updates lag 20–60 minutes behind reality. The burden isn't paperwork — it's manually re-verifying a system that's supposed to do the verifying for them.
External Tool / Workaround
The dispatcher doesn't escalate through official channels — they maintain a running WhatsApp group with drivers and treat that as the real system of record, not the dashboard.
"Every reassignment now costs two actions instead of one, and every status check requires the dispatcher to hold two competing pictures of reality in their head and manually decide which to believe."
The Manual Process
  1. Reassign the route in-app, as designed.
  2. Assume it hasn't landed — no push notification, 8–15 min delay (BUG-2044).
  3. Re-send the same instruction via WhatsApp, duplicating the action.
  4. Wait for a reply, not a status change — the dashboard can't be trusted to confirm receipt.
Journey Map Shift starts→ Open console→ Hunt admin-heavy tabs for live status→ Give up, switch to spreadsheet / phone→ Velocity: live dispatch board on open

09 · Launch

Goal · Conversion

Reduce dispatcher reliance on the WhatsApp workaround by closing the reassignment-notification gap — cut reassignment-to-acknowledgment time to ≤5min median, with no regression in status-lag accuracy or app stability, within the quarter.

Audience · Wave 1

Dispatchers at enterprise accounts already flagged for renewal risk — where the trust gap's business cost is most acute and measurable.

10 · Story

Reflection & Next Steps

FRICTION POINTS

  • The Variant field in the A/B brief was a copy-paste error identical to Control — a small slip that would have invalidated the whole experiment if it had shipped unreviewed.
  • The guardrail and primary metric started conflated ("protect <8–15min" restating the improvement target) — the same root habit later showed up as sample-size and event-definition ambiguity.
  • Section-by-section GTM building without a running document let ownership drift from names back to roles by the final pressure-test.

KEY LEARNINGS

  • Speed metrics and trust metrics are not the same metric — the single most repeated failure mode across the whole exercise.
  • Deliberately deferring a feature (the confirmation loop) creates a predictable, nameable downstream risk — worth pre-writing, not discovering later.
  • A role is not an owner. Any document that outlives one working session needs a check for whether accountability degraded into abstraction.
Aha! Moment

A technically successful launch and a strategically failed one can look identical on the primary metric. Reassignment time hitting ≤5min and holding is unambiguous good news on its own — but paired with a flat WhatsApp-duplication rate, it's evidence the deferred confirmation-loop work isn't optional-later, it's blocking-now. The combination of metrics, not any single one, tells the true story — the moment this stopped being "did the feature ship correctly" and became "did the feature do what it was for."

Next For B2B tools, the workflow at the moment of decision beats feature breadth. Next: instrument the reroute flow and A/B test exception-alert timing to cut time-to-action further.
